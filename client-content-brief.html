Website Content

A simple, complete guide for sharing your school, academy, company or organisation’s content for a ready-to-publish website.

Prepared from the current PEN School System templateSeptember 2026
How to use this document

Send one organised content pack

Please use the checklist below when sending your website information. If a section does not apply to your organisation, write “Not applicable” instead of leaving it blank.

What we need from you

✓ Final text in an editable file
✓ Original, uncompressed images
✓ Logo in SVG/PNG format
✓ Correct names, dates and contact details
✓ Image captions and people’s designations
✓ Page-specific calls to action
✓ Social media and map links
✓ One contact person for approvals

File naming

Use clear names such as home-hero-01.jpg director-message.docx campus-lahore.jpg. Do not send screenshots of text; send the actual copy so it stays sharp and editable.

Recommended delivery: one Google Drive/Dropbox folder with subfolders: 01-Brand, 02-Content, 03-Images, 04-Video, 05-Forms.

Universal image rules

Original files
No WhatsApp-compressed images where possible.
Landscape first
Keep the subject away from extreme edges for responsive crops.
Permission
Confirm consent for identifiable students, staff and parents.
Website Content & Media Brief01
Template overview

Website map at a glance

The current template is built around a strong homepage, short supporting pages, leadership messages, admissions and student-life information.

01 · Home
Hero, mission, vision, highlights, programme, clubs, video, team, LMS and statistics.
02 · About
About organisation, philosophy, journey, policies, FAQs, messages and resource pages.
03 · Curriculum / Services
Academic programme or your main services, process and outcomes.
04 · Admission / Enquiry
Registration, transfer/withdrawal information and online enquiry form.
05 · Student Life / Activities
Clubs, counselling, workshops, training, library and extracurricular activities.
06 · Careers
Vacancies, workplace culture, requirements and application method.
07 · Contact
Address, phone, email, timings, map, social links and admission help.

Website Content & Media Brief02
Homepage · 01

Home page content pack

Template snapshot: homepage hero and opening content modules.

Hero section

Text: headline 4–9 words; supporting line 18–28 words; one button label 2–4 words.

Media: 1 main image or video frame. 1920×900 px landscape; keep key subject centred.

Welcome / mission / vision

Text: welcome heading 3–7 words; mission 60–100 words; vision 35–60 words; 4–6 bullet commitments.

Media: optional logo/icon artwork, SVG or transparent PNG.

Highlights / announcements

Text: 4 items, each 6–14 words, plus destination page or button link.

Media: optional icons; SVG preferred.

Philosophy / programme / curriculum

Text: 3 sections; each 45–75 words. Provide section title and order.

Media: optional timeline icons; 800×800 px if raster.

Website Content & Media Brief03
Homepage · 02

Trust, activity & conversion modules

About / statistics

Text: about paragraph 70–110 words. Provide 3–6 statistics with a number and label, e.g. “12 Campuses”.

Media: 1 supporting image, 1200×900 px landscape.

Clubs / activities

Text: intro 25–45 words; 4–8 items; each title 2–5 words + description 15–30 words.

Media: one image per item, 1200×900 px landscape.

Video section

Text: heading 4–8 words; supporting copy 20–35 words; button label.

Media: YouTube/Vimeo link or MP4, ideally 1920×1080 px, 30–120 seconds.

Team / leadership preview

Text: intro 20–35 words; 3–8 profiles; name, title and bio 35–60 words each.

Media: portrait per person, 1000×1200 px, head-and-shoulders.

LMS / primary CTA

Text: heading 4–8 words; copy 20–35 words; button label; destination URL.

Links: LMS/login, admission, WhatsApp or booking link.

Final statistics / CTA

Text: 3–5 proof points or a short closing message of 25–45 words.

Media: optional brand mark, icon or background texture.

Homepage total: prepare approximately 500–850 words, 8–15 images, 3–6 icons, 1 video and all button links.

Homepage approval checklist

□ Hero crop approved   □ All statistics verified   □ Every button destination confirmed   □ People in photos identified/approved

Website Content & Media Brief04
About & leadership

Tell your story with authority

About organisation

Text: 250–450 words covering origin, purpose, community served, approach and what makes you different.

Media: 2–4 images, 1600×1100 px landscape; optional historic image.

Chairperson / founder message

Text: 250–450 words, written in first person. Add name, designation and date if needed.

Media: formal portrait, 1000×1200 px, plus signature/logo optional.

Director / principal message

Text: 250–450 words, warm and audience-focused. Include current priorities and invitation to connect.

Media: portrait 1000×1200 px; optional campus image.

Philosophy & journey

Text: philosophy 180–300 words; journey timeline 5–8 milestones, each 12–25 words with year.

Media: 2–5 images, 1600×1100 px; logo/marks in SVG.

Writing tone: sincere, specific and human. Avoid generic claims such as “best quality” unless supported by a clear fact, result or recognised achievement.

Website Content & Media Brief06
Admission & forms

Make the next step easy

Registration process

Text: 4–7 numbered steps; each 15–35 words. Include documents, deadlines, fees, assessment/interview and confirmation method.

Media: optional process icons; SVG or 800×800 px PNG.

Withdrawals / transfers

Text: eligibility, notice period, documents, outstanding dues, handover and contact person; 150–250 words.

Media: none required; downloadable form optional.

Online form fields to confirm

□ Full name
□ Mobile / WhatsApp
□ Email
□ City / branch
□ Grade / service required
□ Referral source
□ Query/message
□ Preferred date/time
□ Consent statement
□ Privacy notice link
□ Success message
□ Receiving email/CRM

Required from client: final field names, dropdown options, mandatory fields, receiving email/phone, privacy wording and the exact success/thank-you message.

Important: never send passwords or private student records in the content pack. Provide only public-facing information and approved media.
Website Content & Media Brief07
Student life & activities

Show the experience beyond the core offer

Societies & clubs

Text: introduction 35–60 words; 4–10 clubs; title + 20–35-word description + age/group if relevant.

Media: 1 image per club, 1200×900 px.

Counselling & mentoring

Text: purpose, who it supports, confidentiality boundaries, session format and contact route; 180–300 words.

Media: 1 calm, inclusive image, 1600×1100 px.

Parenting workshop

Text: overview 100–180 words; topics, audience, schedule, registration and facilitator bio 50–80 words.

Media: workshop images, 1600×1100 px.

Teacher training

Text: purpose 100–180 words; modules 4–8; outcomes and enquiry CTA.

Media: 2–4 training images, 1600×1100 px.

Library / extracurricular

Text: facilities, activities, timings, rules and benefits; 120–220 words per page.

Media: 3–6 images per page, 1600×1100 px.

Dynamic detail pages

Text: each detail page: title, intro 30–60 words, body 150–300 words, CTA.

Media: feature image plus gallery if available.

Website Content & Media Brief08
Resources, careers & contact

Complete the trust layer

Policies & guidelines

Text: 6–12 policy summaries, 30–80 words each. Send approved full policy PDFs separately if they must be downloadable.

Links: privacy, safeguarding, terms and complaints route.

FAQs

Text: 8–15 questions with answers of 35–90 words. Include fees, timings, age/eligibility, documents and contact process.

Careers

Text: culture intro 80–140 words; each vacancy: title, location, requirements, responsibilities, deadline and application method.

Media: team/workplace images, 1600×1100 px.

Uniform / books / resources

Text: supplier details, address, phone, timings, purchase instructions and price list link; 100–180 words.

Media: product/guide images, 1200×1200 px square or PDF catalogue.

Contact page data

Official name · address · phone · WhatsApp · email · map pin · opening hours · social links

Send exact spellings: contact information is copied as supplied. Please verify every number, URL, email and map location before approval.
